# MLOps and RAG administrator access decision

The repository owner replied "yes, proceed" to the recommendation to make
MLOps and RAG administrator-only, enforce the policy at the API boundary, and
prove that anonymous callers and regular users are denied. This resolves the
access-control questions deferred from the initial hero/harness review.

The local-showcase MLOps workflow remains sampling, customer addition and
reclustering, charts, and customer results. Both the gateway and Flask API now
require a verified CloudApp administrator before data or job operations. The
health endpoint and preflight do not expose customer operations and remain
available for readiness and browser negotiation.

RAG is an administrator-managed shared corpus. Uploads, document/job reads,
queries, deletion, statistics, and RAG health require a verified administrator
session at both boundaries. Optional user IDs are metadata and administrator
filters, not ownership proof. General AI readiness remains separate. Service
tokens and caller-supplied role headers do not substitute for an administrator
session on these two APIs. Synthetic RAG load checks must also use an admin
session.

RAG orchestration, streaming, parallel tasks, and approval resumption use the
same administrator boundary before accessing the corpus. A resume requires
fresh verified credentials matching the original administrator requester;
approval cannot elevate an original regular user into corpus access.

This decision accepts these two requirements with their existing functional
flows and the new denial scenarios. It does not certify production readiness,
model quality, or external-provider availability. The exact accepted revisions
are bound above and in requirements/baseline.json. The merged reviewed PR
becomes the maintained baseline.
